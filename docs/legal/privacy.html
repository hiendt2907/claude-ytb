Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

YT Content Publisher is a local desktop tool for preparing and publishing short-form videos.

Information we process

When you connect TikTok, the tool processes the authorization information and access credentials needed to publish to the TikTok account you authorize. It also processes video files, captions, hashtags, and publishing settings that you provide.

How information is used

Information is used only to authenticate your account, upload or publish your selected videos, and report publishing status. The tool does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Storage and sharing

Credentials are intended to be stored locally in the application's protected, gitignored secrets storage. Video files and generated metadata remain on your computer unless you choose to upload them to a connected platform. We do not maintain a separate analytics database of your TikTok account.

Third parties

TikTok receives information required to process the publishing request under its own privacy policy and terms. You can revoke the application's access from your TikTok account settings.

Your choices

You may disconnect TikTok, delete locally stored credentials, or stop using the tool at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the repository associated with this application.
